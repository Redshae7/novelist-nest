Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

What we collect

  • Song details — the names, occasion, and memories you type in, used only to write and produce your song.
  • Email — if you provide it, used to deliver your song and (rarely) follow up about your order.
  • Payment — handled entirely by Stripe; we never see or store card numbers.
  • Basic usage analytics — anonymous counters (e.g. how many songs were created per day). No advertising trackers, no third-party analytics scripts, no cookies for tracking.

How your song details are used

Your inputs are sent to our AI providers (for lyric writing and music production) solely to create your song. We don't sell your data or use your stories for advertising.

Sharing

Your song page is accessible to anyone who has its private link — that's what makes it giftable. Don't share the link publicly unless you want the song public.

Retention & deletion

Song records are kept so your purchased songs remain available. Want a song or your email removed? Reply to your receipt email and we'll delete it.

Contact

Privacy questions: reply to any email from us and a human will answer.
